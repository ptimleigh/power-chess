Drop your own sound effects here as MP3 files named after the game's sound events, then list the names (without .mp3) in manifest.json.
Example: manifest.json = ["select", "capture"] uses select.mp3 and capture.mp3 instead of the built-in synth sounds.
Names the game uses: click, move, capture, pickup, spawn, fire, bomb, steal, stun, poison, slash, warp, thud, rewind, win, lose, draw, select, blip.
Keep effects short, trimmed of silence at both ends, and a little quieter than the music.
