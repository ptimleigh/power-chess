Chess Fighter Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Chess Fighter is a chess game. This page explains what information the game and its ads use. In short: there are no accounts, and your game progress stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress: level, XP, trophies, settings, tutorial progress, the game in progress, and your daily puzzle streak.
  • This is stored in the app's local storage on your device only. We do not receive it and cannot see it. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling deletes it.

What we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any account information, and the game has no sign-in.

Ads

The Android app shows ads (full-screen ads between some games, and optional ads you can watch for extra XP or a hint) using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads. This depends on the choices you make.

  • Where required (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change this at any time from How to play, then Ad choices.
  • You can reset or opt out of personalized ads in your device's settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads).
  • Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

The web version at this address shows no ads.

Sharing a replay video

When you make a replay video, it is created on your device. It leaves your device only if you choose to share it, using your phone's share sheet. We do not upload it.

Children

Chess Fighter is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help.
