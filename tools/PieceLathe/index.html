Profile

Tap empty space to add a point, drag a point to move it. Pick Delete or Sharp / smooth, then tap a point. Lathe: the faint mirror shows the other side of the axis. Extrude: draw the side silhouette. Mouse: right-click deletes and double-click toggles a corner.

Motion preview

Turn on Move piece (top of the 3D view) and drag to push the piece around and watch the ribbons trail. With a mouse you can hold Shift and drag instead. Idle settings are saved with the piece.
